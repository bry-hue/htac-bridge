OUR RESEARCH TEAM

About the Team

We are a team of students who worked together to develop this research project. Each member contributed their time, ideas, skills, and knowledge throughout the different stages of the study.

Our goal is to explore how a centralized school information website can help students and parents access important school information in a more convenient, organized, and accessible way.

OUR PROJECT

About This Research

This research explores how a school website can provide students and parents with easier access to school announcements, academic resources, calendars, policies, and other important information.

The project aims to demonstrate how digital information management can improve the accessibility, organization, and convenience of school-related information for the school community.
