WHO WE ARE

Mission, Vision & Philosophy

The guiding principles that shape the community and educational journey of Holy Trinity Academy of Calamba.

OUR FOUNDATION

Guided by Faith, Excellence and Service

We are a school that has created a distance education program to meet the needs of students from the comfort and safety of his/her home. We remain strongly committed to partner with parents to raise up kids who are "maka-Diyos, makatao, makakalikasan, at makabansa."

✦
OUR MISSION

Mission

To partner with parents to commit ourselves to the development of the whole person, by providing a solid-spiritual and educational foundation to shepherd the youth to love and serve God by their very lives.

◈
OUR VISION

Vision

We envision the community of Holy Trinity Academy of Calamba to be lifelong learners who will strive to use and develop our God-given talents towards a life of excellence and worship to Him.

✧
OUR PHILOSOPHY

Philosophy

Holy Trinity Academy of Calamba entrusts and dedicates this school to the Lord. We, including the parents, students, faculty and staff, want to give our best service.
